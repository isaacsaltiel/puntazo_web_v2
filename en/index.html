Puntazo Clips · Mexico

Padel highlights with one button

Puntazo Clips is a padel replay system built in Mexico. Every court has a camera and a button: after a great point, a player presses the button and the last 60 seconds of play show up on puntazoclips.com a couple of minutes later, with the club's logo, ready to download and share. It's free for players and needs no app. Clubs pay for it.

How it works

  1. Play at a club with Puntazo. Each court has its own camera and a QR code that opens that court's clip page. Most courts also have a physical button next to them.
  2. Press the button after the point. Use the Wi-Fi button next to the court or the digital button on your phone. The light on the physical button turns green once the point is saved.
  3. Download and share. A couple of minutes later the clip is on the court's page, with the club's logo and, at some clubs, background music. Send it on WhatsApp or post it to Instagram or TikTok.

You don't have to start recording before the rally. The cameras record all the time, and the button only marks the moment, so you decide which point was worth keeping after you play it. If you forget to press, you can ask for the point by court and approximate time on the recover page, as long as the club's recording still has it.

For club owners

Puntazo installs and runs the whole system: buttons, cameras if the club needs them, a small computer at the club that cuts and uploads the clips, and a web page for every court. The club provides the space, power and an internet connection with decent upload speed.

What the club gets:

  • Free marketing made by its own players. Every clip carries the club's logo, and players share them on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp.
  • A reason to choose your club. Most players have never seen themselves play. Getting their best point on video is something people talk about.
  • Sponsor inventory you can measure. Brands can appear in the clips and on the court pages, and we count every view and click.
  • Live streaming. The same cameras can stream tournaments to YouTube.

We currently operate in the State of Mexico and Mérida, and we're looking for clubs in Mexico City, Guadalajara, Monterrey, Toluca, Cuernavaca and Acapulco. The full details for clubs are on Puntazo para clubes (in Spanish), and we're happy to talk in English.

Preguntas frecuentes

How long does it take for a clip to appear?

Usually a couple of minutes. At BreakPoint we measured 2 minutes and 22 seconds from the button press to the clip being live on the website.

Do players need to install an app or create an account?

No. Clips are watched and downloaded in the phone's browser. Signing in with Google is optional and only needed to save favourites.

Where can I play with Puntazo?

At BreakPoint Indoor Padel in Atizapán de Zaragoza and InterPadel MX in Interlomas (both in the Mexico City metro area, State of Mexico), and at Well Street Pádel & Pickleball in Mérida, Yucatán.

How much does it cost for a club?

A monthly fee based on the number of courts, with no charge per clip or per player. Message us on WhatsApp (+52 220 680 4856) with your number of courts and city for a quote.

Is Puntazo the same as the padel scoreboard apps called Puntazo?

No. Puntazo Clips (puntazoclips.com) is the Mexican highlight-clip system for padel clubs. Other apps and accounts with similar names are unrelated.

Do you run a padel club?

Tell us how many courts you have and where you are. We reply on WhatsApp, in English or Spanish.
