## How it works

<ol class="ct-steps">
<li><strong>Play at a club with Puntazo.</strong> Each court has its own camera and a QR code that opens that court's clip page. Most courts also have a physical button next to them.</li>
<li><strong>Press the button after the point.</strong> Use the Wi-Fi button next to the court or the digital button on your phone. The light on the physical button turns green once the point is saved.</li>
<li><strong>Download and share.</strong> A couple of minutes later the clip is on the court's page, with the club's logo and, at some clubs, background music. Send it on WhatsApp or post it to Instagram or TikTok.</li>
</ol>

You don't have to start recording before the rally. The cameras record all the time, and the button only marks the moment, so you decide which point was worth keeping after you play it. If you forget to press, you can ask for the point by court and approximate time on the [recover page](/recuperar.html), as long as the club's recording still has it.

## For club owners

Puntazo installs and runs the whole system: buttons, cameras if the club needs them, a small computer at the club that cuts and uploads the clips, and a web page for every court. The club provides the space, power and an internet connection with decent upload speed.

What the club gets:

- **Free marketing made by its own players.** Every clip carries the club's logo, and players share them on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp.
- **A reason to choose your club.** Most players have never seen themselves play. Getting their best point on video is something people talk about.
- **Sponsor inventory you can measure.** Brands can appear in the clips and on the court pages, and we count every view and click.
- **Live streaming.** The same cameras can stream tournaments to YouTube.

We currently operate in the State of Mexico and Mérida, and we're looking for clubs in Mexico City, Guadalajara, Monterrey, Toluca, Cuernavaca and Acapulco. The full details for clubs are on [Puntazo para clubes](/para-clubes/) (in Spanish), and we're happy to talk in English.
